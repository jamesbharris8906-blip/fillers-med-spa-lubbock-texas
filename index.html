Questions about dermal fillers

What are dermal fillers made of?

Most fillers are a gel made to sit under the skin. The gel adds volume and holds water. Your provider will tell you which type fits your spot. Different spots need different gels.

How long do fillers last?

It depends on the spot and the type. Lips often last a few months. Cheeks often last longer. Your provider will give you a time frame for your plan before you start.

Do fillers hurt?

Most people feel a pinch and some pressure. Numbing cream can help. A cold pack can help, too. The visit is short. Tell your provider if you feel nervous.

Will my lips look too big?

Not if the plan is light. The goal is a soft, even look. Your provider can start with a small amount. You can add more later if you want. Tell them you want a natural look.

What about swelling after fillers?

Some swelling is normal for a day or two. Lips can swell more than cheeks. A cold pack can help. Skip hard workouts for the rest of the day. Call us if swelling lasts longer than a few days.

Can fillers help under my eyes?

They can help some people. A small amount can help hollows look less deep. Not all under-eye lines fit fillers. Your provider will look at your skin and tell you if it fits.
